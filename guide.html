7 Score Tab — Start Scoring, then Finalize

The Score tab now works in two stages. This is deliberate: it is what stops a round being overwritten or lost.

Stage 1 — check the teams. When you open the Score tab you do not get score boxes straight away. You get the teams listed out, with the men on each one, and a single green ▶ Start Scoring button. Read the teams. If anything is wrong there is a button to go back to the Teams tab and fix it. Once you press Start Scoring the teams are locked for that day.

The app checks the date here. If the day's date is not part of the current week, or is more than a couple of days old, Start Scoring will refuse and tell you to fix it in Manage Days. This catches a wrong date before anyone plays, instead of after the whole round is scored.

Stage 2 — score as teams come in. Press Start Scoring and the score boxes appear. Enter each team's Front, Back and Overall and tap their skins as they finish — you do not have to wait for everyone. A strip across the top shows where you are:

Team One ✓ scored · Team Two waiting · Team Three waiting

Your scores save themselves. Every change is saved about a second after you stop typing. There is no Save button any more because you do not need one. Close the browser, lose signal, or hand the phone to someone else — what you entered is already saved.

Saving is not publishing. Nothing reaches History until you press Finalize. You can save as many times as you like and it cannot touch a finished round. This is the change that stops rounds disappearing.

Finishing the round. Finalize & Publish — Save to History only becomes available once every team has a score. Until then it is greyed out and tells you who you are still waiting on. When you press it you get a confirmation, and if no skins have been recorded for the whole round it will ask whether that is right — there is normally at least one.

Once published, the button is gone. It is replaced by ✓ Round saved with the date. There is nothing left to press twice, and the day drops off the Score tab because it is now in History.

Skins surplus note: until you tap at least one skin, the payout preview shows the full skins pot as a "rounding surplus." This is normal and clears once skins are entered.

If something is wrong mid-round: use Cancel Day at the bottom of the Score tab. It asks you to confirm and then makes you type CANCEL, so it cannot happen by accident. No round is saved, no attendance is recorded, and nothing goes to the tournament fund.

Gone from this tab: Save Preview / Update Preview (the app saves on its own now) and Edit Scores (scores stay editable right up until you press Finalize, so there is nothing to reopen).
