For everyone

1Signing inMember

Open the app and tap Member Sign In. Your PIN is the last 4 digits of your phone number. Find your name, enter those four digits, and you're in.

The app remembers you on that device, so you only do this once per phone.

Two men with the same last 4? The app spots it and asks which of you it is. Pick your name and carry on.

2Signing up to playMember

The Week tab is the front page. Each day we're playing gets a card showing the date, the tee time, the weather, and who has signed up so far.

  1. Tap the day you want to play.
  2. Check your tees and handicap — they're pre-filled from last time. Fix them if they're wrong, because teams are built off those numbers.
  3. Confirm. Your name appears in the list straight away.

Changed your mind? Tap your own name in the list and remove yourself. Do it early if you can — once teams are built, taking someone out means the admin has to rebuild.

If the day is full you'll join the waiting list instead. If someone drops out, the first man on the list moves up automatically and the admins are told.

3Bringing a guestMember

When you sign up you can add a guest at the same time. You'll need their name and their course handicap for the tees they'll play.

Your guest counts as a player: they go into a team, they pay in, and they can win. You're responsible for their money.

4Seeing your teamMember

Teams appear on the Teams tab once an admin publishes them — you'll get a notification the moment they do. Until then the tab tells you they aren't out yet.

Teams are balanced by combined handicap, so every group has a fair shout. You'll see your team, the other teams, and the tee-off order.

Faces, not just names. If a man has added a photo it shows next to his name. Tap any photo to see it bigger — handy when you're looking for someone on the first tee.

5Results and payoutsMember

Scores go in as groups come off the course. When the round is finished and published you'll get a notification, and the Score tab shows the full breakdown: front nine, back nine, overall, skins, and exactly what each man is owed.

Older rounds live on the History tab. Standings shows the season money list, and Attend shows who has played how often.

6How the money worksMember

Everyone puts in $25. It splits the same way every week, based on how many are playing:

Front nine$5 a man
Back nine$5 a man
Overall$5 a man
Skins$10 a man
Tournament fund$1 a man, taken out of skins
Total in$25 a man

So with 20 players: $100 on the front, $100 on the back, $100 overall, and $200 in skins — less $20 to the tournament fund, leaving $180 of skins to be won.

Payouts are rounded down to whole dollars, and any odd change left over goes to the tournament fund as well. The fund line is shown on both the scoring screen and in History, so you can always see where it stands.

That $1 a week is what pays for the season tournament. You've been contributing to it since the first round of the year.

7Your profile and photoMember

Tap your own picture — either at the top of the screen, or next to your name on the Week or Teams tab — to open your profile. From there you can:

  • Add or change your photo
  • Set your default tees and handicap, so sign-ups are pre-filled
  • Add your email address
  • Turn notifications on
  • Write the short welcome message that greets you when you sign in

Photos are cropped square. Frame your face in the middle and it'll look right everywhere in the app.

8NotificationsMember

Worth turning on. You'll get an alert when:

  • A new week opens for sign-ups
  • Teams are published
  • A lottery tee time is finally set
  • Results are posted
  • A day gets cancelled

To switch them on, open your profile and use the Notifications row. The bell at the top of the screen shows an amber dot while they're off.

On iPhone this only works if you add the app to your Home Screen first. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then open the app from that icon and turn notifications on there. It will not work in Safari.

9Putting it on your phoneMember

It isn't in the App Store — it installs straight from the web and behaves the same way.

iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

It then opens full-screen with no browser bar, and notifications work properly.

10The season tournamentMember

The Tourney tab covers the end-of-season event. It shows the dates, the pot as it stands, who's in, and what you personally owe.

Entry is $45, less $1 for every round you've played this season. Play more Fridays and you owe less. Your exact figure is on that tab.

When invitations go out you'll see a banner until you answer. Tap it, choose which days you can play, confirm your handicap and tees, and you're entered. You can change your answer any time before the deadline.

One entry fee covers the whole tournament — the same $45 whether you play one day or both.

For admins

Three extra tabs appear if you're an admin: Manage, Email and Admin. Everything below happens in those, or on the Teams and Score tabs.

The Scoring Admin role is for the rare Friday when no admin is playing — somebody needs to put the cards in without being handed the keys to everything.

Showing someone the app

There are two view-only codes, both set in the portal under App Settings. Whoever uses them can navigate freely and cannot change a single thing — every save, publish and delete is dead, and no notifications can be sent.

  • Preview code — shows the app as a member sees it.
  • Admin preview code — shows every admin screen, still completely read-only.
The admin preview shows real data, including the roster with email addresses. Read-only, but visible. Think before you hand that code out.

12Setting up the weekAdmin

Manage → Manage Days. For each day you're playing:

  1. Turn the day on and set the date.
  2. Set the tee time, or mark it as a lottery if you don't know it yet.
  3. Add any extra tee times for information.
The date is the single most important field on this screen. A stale date once filed a round under the previous week and wiped the round that was already there. The app now refuses to start scoring on a date that isn't part of the current week, or is more than two days old — but it's far easier to set it correctly than to explain the refusal later.

When the lottery result comes in, come back and set the real time. Everyone gets a notification automatically.

13Building teamsAdmin

Once sign-ups are in, go to the Teams tab and pick the day.

  1. Build — the app draws teams balanced by combined handicap, honouring pairing requests and do-not-pair rules.
  2. Adjust — drag men between teams, add a late arrival, remove a no-show. Rebuild as often as you like; every previous line-up is saved.
  3. Publish — teams go live, the day locks to new sign-ups, and everyone gets a notification.
This is the phase where changes belong. Nothing here is risky — rebuild ten times if you want.

14Scoring a roundAdmin

The Score tab works in two stages, and the split is deliberate — it's what stops a round being lost.

Stage one — check the teams, then start

Open the Score tab and you won't see score boxes yet. You'll see the teams laid out and one green Start Scoring button.

Read the teams. If something's wrong there's a button to go back and fix it. Press Start Scoring and the teams lock for the day — nobody can rebuild or erase them mid-round.

The date is checked here. If it's wrong you're told now, before anyone tees off, instead of after the whole round is scored.

Stage two — score as groups come in

Enter each team's front, back and overall, and tap their skins, as they finish. You don't wait for everyone. A strip across the top shows where you are:

Team One ✓ scored · Team Two waiting · Team Three waiting

Your scores save themselves about a second after you stop typing. There's no Save button because you don't need one. Close the browser, lose signal, hand the phone to someone else — what you entered is already safe.
Saving is not publishing. Nothing reaches History until you press Finalize. Save as often as you like; it cannot touch a finished round.

Finishing

Finalize & Publish only lights up once every team has a score — until then it's greyed out and tells you who you're waiting on. Press it and you get a confirmation; if no skins have been recorded for the whole round it asks whether that's right.

Then the round goes to History, attendance is recorded, payouts are calculated, and everyone gets a notification.

Once published, the button is gone. Not greyed out — gone. It's replaced by Round saved and the date. There is nothing left to press twice.

15When something goes wrongAdmin

SituationWhat to do
Teams wrong, not startedUse the button on the Score screen to go back to Teams and fix them.
Teams wrong, already startedStop Scoring — unlock teams at the bottom of the Score tab. Sign-ups, teams and any scores you've entered are all kept. Press Start Scoring again when you're done.
Rained offCancel the day from Manage Days. That clears the sign-ups and notifies everyone — right for a washout, wrong for a team mix-up.
Wrong dateFix it in Manage Days, then start scoring.
Wrong score publishedOpen the round in History and edit it. The original is kept.
Round deleted by mistakeIt's in the Deleted list in History. Recover it.
Nothing is ever destroyed. Every version of the teams is kept, every score save is kept, and anything removed goes to a recoverable trash rather than being erased. Mistakes are corrections, not losses.

16Ending the weekAdmin

End Week & Start Fresh sits at the very bottom of the Admin tab, on its own, deliberately out of the way.

It archives any day that still has data, clears the week, and opens the next one. You have to type END to confirm.

Finalise your rounds first. End Week will archive a day that was never scored, and that's a round you'd have to reconstruct by hand.

Weekly notes and the events line carry over to the new week. Everything else starts clean.

17Common questions

I can't sign in.

Your PIN is the last 4 digits of the phone number we have for you. If it's an old number, ask an admin to check the roster.

The app looks out of date.

Close it completely — swipe it away rather than backgrounding it — and reopen. If you're in a browser, pull down to refresh.

Why can't I see Stats?

Stats visibility is set by the Master. Some sections are admin-only by design.

I signed up but I'm not on a team.

Either teams aren't published yet, or you signed up after they were built. Speak to an admin — you can still be added.

Why is the tee time blank?

It's a lottery day and the result isn't in. You'll be notified the moment it's set.

Do I have to pay through the app?

No. The app works out what's owed and what's won. Money changes hands the way it always has.

Why does a round show players but no teams?

Two rounds from late August and early September lost their team line-ups to a bug that has since been fixed. The scores and payouts are intact, and the players are shown from the attendance record. Only who played with whom is gone.

Something's wrong and it isn't covered here.

Tell Jared. If it's a round or the money, tell him before the next week is started — it's far easier to fix while the week is still open.
