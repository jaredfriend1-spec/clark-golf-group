For the Admins

The app does the heavy lifting. You just drag, drop, and push the buttons.

★ Signing In as Admin

Admins sign in exactly like members — register your phone number using “Need to set up your login?” on the landing page, then sign in with your last 4 digits. The app recognises your admin status automatically and unlocks the Admin tab. No separate password needed.

Admins must register first

Before deploying to members, make sure all admins have registered their phone numbers. Otherwise you won’t be able to access the Admin tab after deploy.

Two sign-up buttons for admins

On the Week tab, admins see two buttons at the bottom of each day card instead of the usual one:

+ Sign me up — green, instantly pre-fills your own name (same fast experience as a regular member).
+ Sign up someone — blue, opens the full roster picker so you can sign up any player on their behalf, set their tees and HCP, add a tee preference, or create a match pairing for them.

Granting admin access

Admin status is set in the Roster section. Find the member, tap Edit, and toggle the Admin flag on. They’ll get ⭐ on the welcome screen when they next sign in.

🔒 Master Admin protection

Certain admins (Jared and Clark) are flagged as Master Admins in Firebase. Their phone number and admin status are completely locked in the app UI — no other admin can change them. The only way to update a Master Admin’s phone number is through self-registration on the landing page, which requires physical access to their phone. This prevents anyone from accidentally or intentionally locking out a core admin.

● Quick note on navigation

The bottom tab bar uses colored icons to help you find things fast — each tab has its own color when active: Week (green), Teams (blue), Score (amber), History (purple), Email (cyan), Admin (orange).

1 Set Up Your Days

The Admin tab opens straight to Manage Days. Four days are available: Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and Monday (for holiday weekends). Click any day to run the setup wizard — set the tee time, lock sign-ups, and build teams. Day checklists track your progress. Only activate the days you’re actually playing.

Set up Saturday

2 Adding a Guest (Admin)

In Manage Existing Sign-Ups For The Week, tap + Add Guest. Fill in:

Guest name — their full name.
Guest of — select the member from the roster dropdown.
Tees & Handicap — standard fields.
Request pairing — optional checkbox to request guest and member on the same team.

Guests never touch the roster

Every guest is tied to that week’s sign-up data only. When the week ends, they’re gone. The roster stays clean with permanent members only.

3 Copy Player List Per Day

On the Week tab, each day has a Copy Players button (admin only, next to the tee time controls). Tap it to copy a clean numbered list of all signed-up players for that day — name, tees, and handicap — ready to paste anywhere. The button flashes green and shows “✓ Copied!” for confirmation.

7 Smart Team Mixing

The auto-generate algorithm checks your round history and avoids putting the same players together week after week — while keeping teams balanced by handicap. Gets smarter the more rounds you play.

8 Fine-Tuning & Reordering

Drag the handle (⋮) next to any player to move them between teams. Use the Group Dropdown in each team header to set the tee-off order. Works in both auto and manual modes.

A small ✕ button appears on each player row in edit mode — tap it to remove a player from a team. They drop instantly into the gold Unassigned Players banner below, where you can place them in any other team with one tap.

9 The Blind Draw

Uneven number of players? The app flags the short group with a “Blind Draw TBD” banner. You can assign a draw player before publishing, or leave it blank — both are fine. Sort it out on the tee box if needed.

10 Waiting List — Admin Flow

The waiting list is visible on the Teams tab. When you’re ready to add someone, tap “Add to Round”. The player moves from waitlist into sign-ups, the day unlocks, and teams unpublish — but existing teams stay intact. You don’t have to regenerate everything.

The new player appears in a gold “Not yet in a team” banner at the top of the team builder. Tap “Add to team →” and choose which team to drop them into. Re-publish when done.

11 Publish & Lock

Hit Publish & Lock Teams to assign official names (Team One, Team Two…) and lock the roster. Use Copy teams to paste formatted groups into Gmail or iMessage.

12 Edit or Reset Published Teams

Edit Teams — unlocks existing teams for rearranging. Drag players, use the ✕ button to remove someone from a team, then re-publish. No regenerating needed.

Reset Teams — clears all teams and starts over with the current sign-up list. Available in two places:

• When teams are published — a red Reset Teams button appears next to Edit Teams.
• When teams are built but not yet published — a red ✕ Clear Teams button appears at the top right of the team list.

⚠ Stale player warning

If a player removes themselves from sign-ups after teams have been published, a red warning banner appears at the top of the Teams tab showing exactly whose name is stale. A Reset Teams Now button lets you clear and regenerate immediately with the correct player list. Sign-ups are always preserved — only the team assignments are cleared.

Weekly Email

Generate a polished weekly update email and copy it straight into Gmail. No email service needed — the app builds it for you.

1 Open the Email Tab

Tap the envelope icon in the admin nav. The tab shows only upcoming play days — past days are excluded automatically. A status strip at the top shows which days are included and whether teams are locked.

2 Edit the Subject Line

The subject auto-generates from the current week dates (e.g., “Clark Golf — Week of May 9–11 Update”). Edit it inline to customize before copying.

3 Add Day Notes

Each day (Friday through Monday) always appears in the notes section, even if that day isn’t active yet. Days not set up yet show a small “not active this week” label. Notes are up to 1,000 characters, resizable, auto-save as you type, and appear as a gold callout in the email and on the Week tab.

Note from the admin 2-man best ball today. $5 skins on the side. Meet at the range by 7:45.

4 Upcoming Events

A large text block (resizable) for tournament schedules, event announcements, and anything not tied to a specific day. Use the B, I, U toolbar to format selected text. This block persists across weeks — it won’t clear when you end the week. Clear it manually when no longer needed.

5 Preview Email

Tap the “Preview Email” button to open a full preview in a popup modal — exactly what recipients will see, including teams, sign-up lists, admin notes, and upcoming events. Close it with the ✕ button when done.

6 Copy & Send

Two buttons fixed at the bottom of the screen:

Copy Formatted — copies the full styled HTML email. Paste into Gmail and it renders perfectly — proper 600px width, gradient header, team cards with shadows, gold note callout, app link.

Copy Plain Text — clean text-only version with dividers and aligned columns. Perfect for iMessage, group texts, or email clients that strip HTML.

Season Attendance

A separate tracker that follows every player across the whole season — rounds played and the year-end tournament pot.

1 Automatic Tracking

Every time a round is moved to history or the week is closed out, player names are automatically pushed to attendance. No duplicate counting — if the same date is pushed twice, it replaces rather than duplicates.

2 Season Standings

A searchable leaderboard ranked by rounds played. Tap any player to see every date they played. Gold, silver, and bronze badges for the top three.

3 Year-End Tournament Pot

Tracks $1 per player per round automatically, always visible in the header.

4 Admin Controls

Admins can log rounds manually — pick a date, select players from the full roster, add guests by name. Edit or delete existing rounds. Import attendance data from CSV for bulk loading.

5 End Season & Archive

At year-end, archive the entire season. Archived seasons stay viewable from pill buttons in the header. A separate Clear All Data option permanently deletes everything — requires typing “DELETE” to confirm.
