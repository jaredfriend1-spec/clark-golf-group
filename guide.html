How It Works

Install the App on Your Phone

The Clark Golf app works like a native app — no App Store needed. Tap a button and it lives on your home screen, opens full-screen, no browser chrome.

1 Android (Chrome)

When you open the site, a green banner appears at the top. Tap the big green "Install App on Your Phone" button. Chrome shows a native popup — tap Install. Done.

2 iPhone (Safari)

You must use Safari — it won't work from Chrome on iPhone.

Already installed?

🔔 Turn On Push Notifications

Once the app is on your home screen, open it from that icon and tap the bell at the top (next to Sign out), then Allow. The bell turns green — you'll get a buzz when teams are posted, results are in, a day is cancelled, and more. A "Get notified" card on the Week tab offers the same thing. iPhone note: notifications only work from the installed home-screen app (iOS 16.4+), not from Safari directly.

1 First Time? Set Up Your Login

Tap "Create or Update Your Sign-In" on the landing page. Find your name in the roster, enter your full phone number, confirm it — the last 4 digits become your PIN. You only do this once.

The app remembers you

Your sign-in stays active until you sign out. Close the app and come back — you're still in.

2 Signing In (after setup)

Tap Member Sign In. Select your name from the dropdown, enter your 4-digit PIN, and tap Continue.

3 Continuing as Guest

Not a member? Tap Continue as Guest. You'll land on the Week tab where you can browse the schedule and sign up under your own name as a guest of a member.

🙋 Not a Member Yet?

Tap "Not a member yet? Request to join" on the sign-in page — name, phone, email, and who you know. An admin reviews it and sets you up with a PIN.

Your Profile

Tap your photo or initials in the top-right of any screen to open your profile. Everything here is yours to manage — no admin needed.

1 Photo & Login Card

Upload a photo (drag to position, scroll or buttons to zoom, then "Use photo"). Set a short welcome message that shows on your login flash card. Pick a card theme (Default, Gold, Green, or Purple) — the color shows when you sign in.

2 Default Tees & Handicap

Set the tees and handicap you usually play. These pre-fill your sign-up form automatically, so your next sign-up is just one tap if nothing's changed.

You can still override either one per-round if your handicap changes or you want to play different tees for a specific day. Past sign-ups are never affected — history stays exactly as it was.

3 Email & PIN

Update your email address. Change your sign-in PIN by entering a new phone number twice — the last 4 digits become your PIN. The current PIN is hidden behind a "👁 Show" button so prying eyes don't spot it.

Stats summary

The top of your profile shows your rounds played, total money won, and net (+/−) for the season. Read-only — just a quick personal snapshot.

Name changes

Names are admin-only because they're tied to your historical records (sign-ups, teams, wins, attendance). Ask an admin if you need a name fix.

Announcements

Admins can post announcements that appear at the top of the app for everyone — tournament alerts, schedule changes, new features, etc.

1 What You'll See

When a new announcement is posted, you'll see one of two styles next time you open the app (or instantly if you're already in it):

Banner — a colored strip at the top with the icon, title, and message. Tap the ✕ to dismiss. Tap the message to expand if it's long.

Modal — a centered popup for important messages. Tap Got it to dismiss.

Categories: 🏆 Tournament · 🎉 New Feature · 📅 Schedule · ⚠️ Important · 💬 General. Each has its own color so you know at a glance what kind of message it is.

Dismissed forever

Most announcements only show once — dismiss and you'll never see them again. A few may reset each session or auto-expire after a set number of days. Either way, you can always dismiss to clear it from your view.

New: some announcements also arrive as a push notification on your phone (if notifications are on) — the admin chooses that when posting.

For the Players

No more messy email chains. Sign up in seconds and see the results instantly.

1 Sign Up for a Day

On the Week tab, tap Sign up on any active day. The modal opens with your name pre-filled, plus your default tees and handicap from your Profile (see Profile section below). Adjust if needed and tap Sign me up — done.

Going alone with no extras? That's it — one screen, one tap.

Sign me up

2 Bringing Guests?

In the sign-up modal you'll see two buttons at the bottom: + Bring a guest and + Playing a match? Tap Bring a guest to open a focused screen for the guest's name, tees, handicap, and whether you want to be paired on the same team.

After adding a guest, you'll see them as a clean card on the main screen. Tap + Another guest to add more — up to 3 guests total, so one person can sign up a full 4-person team in a single flow.

Pair requests

Each guest has its own "Pair with me on a team?" toggle. A green "Pair req" badge shows on the guest's row if pairing was requested. The host shows "Host of [Guest Name]" — or "Host of Guest1, Guest2 & Guest3" if multiple.

Editing or removing guests

Tap Edit next to your own row — you'll see a card view of your sign-up with each guest as their own card. Tap a guest's Edit button to update their info, or the trash icon to remove them. You can also add another guest from this view.

All changes save together when you tap Save changes. Cancel to discard.

3 Match Play

Playing a formal head-to-head match (a real competition with someone)? Tap + Playing a match? at the bottom of the sign-up modal. Pick your opponent from the dropdown — if they're not signed up yet, you'll be asked for their tees and handicap so they can be auto-added when you save.

The match shows up as a card with "Match play vs [Opponent]" and a "Pending admin approval" note. Admins approve before teams are built — opponents are placed on the same team.

This is NOT a pairing request

Match play is for actual competitions. Don't use it to just "play with a friend" — the app builds teams to balance handicaps. If you just want to play together, the team builder usually puts familiar players together over time anyway.

4 Tee Time Preference

Need the first or last group? In the sign-up modal, use the Tee time preference picker to choose First tee, Last tee, or No preference. The admin reviews and approves before building teams.

5 Removing a Sign-Up

Tap the red Remove button next to your name on the Week tab. You can remove yourself, any guest you brought, and any match partner you added. Admins can remove anyone.

6 Sign-Ups Locked? Join the Waiting List

When sign-ups are closed, tap "Join Waiting List". Your name appears in a gold Waiting List section. The admin can add you to the round from the Teams tab. Tap Remove to leave the list anytime.

🌧 Cancelled Days

For the Admins

The app does the heavy lifting. You just set up, publish, and close out.

★ Admin Roles

⭐ Master Admin (Jared)
Full access to everything — all tabs, Stats dashboard, Admin Portal. Cannot be changed by other admins.
🛡️ Super Admin
Sees Email, Admin, and all player tabs. Manages days, teams, roster, notes, scores. Has portal access with a separate password.
📊 Scoring Admin
Player tabs only + Score tab unlocked. Can enter scores and publish results. Cannot see Email, Admin, or Stats. Easy to toggle on/off week to week.
Two sign-up buttons for admins

+ Sign me up — green, pre-fills your own name. + Sign up someone — blue, opens the full roster picker.

Managing admin roles

Done through the Admin Portal (portal.html). See the Admin Portal section below.

⛳ Game Modes — Team or Individual (Stableford)

Every day plays a game mode. The group default is Regular Team Game — new days wear it automatically, no clicks needed. Days can each play a different game: Friday team, Saturday individual, Sunday team.

Where modes come from

Templates are created and managed only in the Admin Portal → Game Modes (buy-in, fund, pots, skins, and for individual games: ranking, paid spots, tie rule). A one-day custom can be built in the app during day setup — it applies to that day only and clears at End Week.

Setting a day's game (admins)

Two places: the day setup panel in Manage Week (current game shown in the colored box; "Switch to a saved game" or configure a custom), or tap the game pill on the Week tab day card for a quick picker. Changing the game never touches sign-ups. Once scores exist, the mode locks (🔒).

Members

Tap the game pill on any day to see what's being played and the money: buy-in, fund, pots — or for individual games, the ranking and paid spots.

🏌️ Individual Days — Groups & Stableford Scoring

On an individual day, the Teams tab becomes Groups — just who plays together and who tees off first. No handicap balancing, no team slots.

Building groups (admins)

Build groups deals everyone randomly into even groups (6 players → 3+3, 7 → 4+3, 10 → 4+3+3). Guest pairs stay together; first-tee requests land in Group 1, last-tee in the last group. Shuffle groups re-deals; drag anyone to rearrange. Groups can hold 5. Publish Groups when set — Group 1 goes off first.

Scoring (Stableford)

The Score tab shows one row per player: Stableford points and a target (from the roster, editable per round — default 36). Ranking is by the mode's setting: net to target (points minus target) or raw total. If the mode has individual skins, tap − / + per player.

Payouts

Collected − tournament fund (a toggle — turn it off for real tournaments) − skins pot = the payout pool, split across the mode's paid spots. Every spot is editable; the app blocks Finalize until money in = money out. Ties split the pooled spot money evenly; all rounding goes to the tournament fund.

Finalize works the same as team days

One button saves scores, payouts, and the day's full game rules to History. Changing a template later never changes a finished round.

1 Set Up Your Days — Admin Tab

The Admin tab opens to Manage Days. Tap Set up [Day] to activate it — the wizard walks through tee time, locking sign-ups, and team building. An inline checklist shows progress per day (⚡ 2/4 or ✓ 4/4).

Set up Saturday

★ Posting Announcements

Below Manage Days in the Admin tab is the Announcements section. Tap + New announcement to compose a message visible to all members.

Pick a category (Tournament, New Feature, Schedule, Important, General), enter a title (60 chars) and message (400 chars), and choose how it displays:

Banner — colored strip at the top, dismissable.
Modal — full popup with "Got it" button, forces attention.

Dismissal options:

• Stays until each user dismisses it — they tap ✕ once, it's gone forever for them.
• Resets every time the app opens — comes back next session even if dismissed.
• Auto-removes for everyone after X days — admin sets a cutoff date, users can still dismiss early.

Pause, edit, or delete

Active announcements show in the admin list with Pause / Edit / Delete buttons. When editing, check "Re-notify everyone" to reset all dismissals — useful when you've made a meaningful update and want everyone to see it again.

The same Announcements tab is also available in the Portal — admins can post from either place. All posts are real-time: members see them within a second of publishing.

2 Build Teams

Auto-generate sorts by handicap into balanced A/B/C/D groups. Manual build lets you hand-pick each player. Drag the handle (⋮) to move players between teams. The algorithm checks history to avoid repeating the same pairings.

Slots always re-sort

After any move, A/B/C/D reassigns automatically by handicap. You can't accidentally put a low HCP in slot D.

3 Approvals Before Building

Before auto-generate, any pending requests appear for review:

Tee time requests — approve/deny first/last group.
Guest pairing requests — approve to keep guest with their member.
Match pairing requests — approve to place opponents on the same team.

4 Balance Button

Available before and after publishing. Tap ⚖️ Balance to see the current handicap spread and the top swap suggestions to improve it. Shows current spread, projected new spread, and up to three swap options. Apply one at a time — re-check after each.

Constraints respected

Guest pairs, match partners, and tee preference players are never suggested for swaps — they stay locked.

5 Lock Sign-Ups

On each day card in the Week tab, the Lock sign-ups button closes the day to new sign-ups. Once locked, members see "Join Waiting List" instead of "Sign me up." Admins can unlock any time.

6 Publish & Lock Teams

Hit Publish & Lock. Teams get official names (Team One, Team Two…) and are visible to all members. Copy teams pastes a clean plain-text version into iMessage or Gmail — includes day header, tee time, player list with slots, any match/tee/guest badges, and a matches summary at the bottom.

Clear teams — appears before publishing. Wipes the current draft and starts fresh — sign-ups are preserved.
Reset Teams — appears after publishing. Unpublishes and clears teams so you can rebuild from scratch.

Promote from Waitlist

In the Teams tab, a gold Waiting List panel shows anyone on the waitlist for that day. Tap Add to Round to move them into sign-ups and unlock the day for rebuilding.

7 Score Tab — Score & Finalize

Enter team scores (Front, Back, Overall) and tap Skins buttons. The app calculates the pot, splits ties, and shows exact dollar payouts. Once all scores are entered a 🏆 Winnings Summary appears with team totals and per-player share. Payment tracking lets you mark who has paid with one tap.

Skins surplus note: Until you tap at least one skin, the payout preview shows the full skins pot as a "rounding surplus." This is normal — it clears automatically once skins are entered.

When the round is done, two buttons close it out:

Finalize Round — Save to History — the one-step finish. Saves the scores, calculates payouts, and moves the round into History in a single tap (with a confirmation). This is the normal way to end every round — players view the results in the History tab.
Save Preview (not finalized) — optional. Saves and shows the payouts without finalizing, so you can double-check the numbers first. A preview is not in History yet — remember to hit Finalize when you're happy with it.

One step, no limbo

Finalize replaces the old two-step "Save & Publish → Move to History" flow. A finalized round is in History immediately — there's no in-between state where a scored round can be lost.

Edit Scores — reopens a saved preview for corrections before finalizing.

✎ Fixing a Round Already in History

Tap Edit on any round in the History tab. The round opens in the Score tab for corrections — and the original entry stays safely in History the whole time. An amber banner on the Score tab reminds you you're editing a past round.

Two ways out:

Finalize Round — saves your corrections and replaces the old History entry with the updated one (the swap is instant — the round is never missing from History).
Cancel Edit — keep History version — discards your changes and leaves the original round in History exactly as it was. Use this if you tapped Edit by accident.

While editing

The round shows in both History (the original) and the Score tab (your working copy) until you Finalize or Cancel. The Teams tab may show a "Teams are out of date" warning on an edited round — ignore it and do not tap Reset Teams; just Finalize or Cancel from the Score tab.

8 Weekly Notes & Announcements

In the Admin tab under Weekly Notes & Announcements, add per-day notes and upcoming events (with B/I/U formatting). Notes appear as a gold callout on the Week tab for members. Upcoming Events persist across End Week — clear manually when done.

9 Roster Management

Under Roster in the Admin tab: edit names, set/reset PINs, hide/restore players, add new players. The Hidden Players section shows anyone you've archived — restorable at any time.

Phone number format

Enter any format — the app strips everything and stores only the last 4 digits as the PIN.

10 Activity Log

The Activity Log (Admin tab, above End Week) records every meaningful action — sign-ups, removals, team publishes, day setup, roster changes — with timestamps and who did it.

★ Stats Tab

Accessible from the Stats tab (second nav row). Master Admin sees the full dashboard — 14 collapsible sections of season intelligence:

Season overview · Avg players by day · Scoring analytics · Pot analytics · Money stats (full player ledger, same as Standings with round breakdown) · Top rounds played · Handicap trends · Tee stats · Player requests · Team chemistry · Member engagement · Sign-up timing · Activity log · Login activity

Access control

Master Admin can open Stats to all members or specific players via the Admin tab. Members with access see a public view of the stats. Lock to nobody to keep it admin-only. All 14 sections filterable by month.

11 End of Week — Start the Next Week

Tap End Week & Start Fresh at the bottom of the Admin tab. Type END to confirm. The board resets for next week, attendance updates, and Upcoming Events persist — only day notes and subject line clear.

Since each round is finalized to History from the Score tab as it finishes, End Week is now just the week rollover — it isn't how scores get to History anymore.

Safety net

If a scored day was somehow never finalized (e.g., left as a preview), End Week automatically sweeps it into History before resetting — a scored round can't be wiped by the rollover. But don't rely on this: Finalize each round from the Score tab as your normal habit.

End Week & Start Fresh

📅 Day Setup Panel (Manage Tab)

One card per day: Game, Tee time, More tee times (dropdown picks or Quick-fill consecutive times — they pair with Groups 1, 2, 3… and follow reorders), Day note, and the Sign-ups lock. For days you're not running, "Show note on Week tab" pins a note without activating the day — the editor opens right there.

🌧 Cancel vs Remove

Cancel (rainouts): type CANCEL, optional reason — members get a push, sign-ups clear, a cancelled card stays on the Week tab, and everything is logged forever in Admin → Cancelled Days. Remove quietly deactivates — no log, no banner.

🙋 Join Requests & ⚠ Alerts

Join requests light a red badge on Admin and push to admins — Approve creates roster entry + PIN in one step (app or portal). Post-publish drop-outs push to admins and appear in the "Needs attention" banner with Dismiss.

⚙️ App Settings & 📣 Push Announcements

Admin → App Settings: guest/pairing/match/tee-preference switches and max guests — same as the portal, no portal needed. Announcements can check "Send as push notification" to buzz everyone.

Admin Portal

A separate management tool at portal.html for running the group behind the scenes. Master Admin and Super Admins with a portal password can access it.

1 What's in the Portal?

Dashboard — active days, player counts, tournament pot, system health, Firebase inspector (master only), activity log, login log.

Week — manage days and sign-ups, day checklists, weekly notes, End Week (master only).

Email — same email builder as the main app.

Roster — segmented into Active (has PIN), Inactive (no PIN), and Hidden. Full edit: name, email, tee, handicap, set/reset PIN.

Access — manage admin roles. Add admins, switch between Super Admin and Scoring Admin, remove access. Set portal passwords for Super Admins.

History — view and manage completed rounds. Delete moves to trash (recoverable from main app).

Attend — log rounds, edit attendance, view standings.

Season — end-of-season tools (coming soon). Master only.

Settings — Firebase auth setup, tournament fund balance, master password. Master only.

2 Managing Admin Roles

Go to the Access tab in the portal. Search for a player → tap + Add as Scoring Admin. Change role with the per-person dropdown (Super Admin / Scoring Admin). Remove with the ✕ button. Set a portal password for Super Admins to give them portal access.

New admins default to Scoring Admin

Actively promote to Super Admin if you want them to have full access.

3 Accessing the Portal

Tap Admin Portal → at the bottom of the app landing page. Sign in with the master password or your portal password.

Shared Firebase database

Changes in the portal write to the same database as the main app. Add an admin in the portal — they see their new access in the main app immediately.
