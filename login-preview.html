Member Login — Concept Preview
💡 What this is
This is a preview of the new login front-end we're considering adding to the Clark Golf app.

Nothing else changes. The Week tab, Teams, Scoring, History, Email — all of that stays exactly the same. This is purely about how you get into the app and how the app knows who you are once you're in.

Right now anyone can sign up as anyone. With this, each member enters their club member number on a keypad — the app finds your name and logs you in automatically. No typing your name. No picking from a dropdown. Just your number and you're in.
🏌️ What changes for you
Before: Open app → tap "Sign up" → scroll through 100 names to find yours → pick days → confirm.

After: Open app → enter your member number once → app remembers you → next time just open and you're already logged in. Tap "Sign up" → your name is already filled in → just pick your days.

That's it. Faster, cleaner, and the app actually knows who you are.
⚠️ Important — This is design only
The demo below shows only the login flow — the number pad, auto-identification, and the guest path. It is not connected to any real data. Use the test PINs below to try each scenario. The full app build comes after we agree on the design.
